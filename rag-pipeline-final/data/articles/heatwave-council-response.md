title: Council approves emergency cooling fund after heat emergency declaration
date: 2026-08-12
author: Clark Kent
section: City
publication: Daily Planet
visibility: public
---
The city council voted unanimously on Monday to release 2 million dollars from its emergency reserve fund to cover the cost of extended cooling center hours and additional staffing during the ongoing heat emergency.

Council members said the funding would cover overtime for city workers staffing the eight cooling centers, additional bottled water distribution, and a wellness-check program targeting elderly residents living alone.

"We budget for emergencies so we're not scrambling when one actually happens," said council member Diana Prince, who introduced the measure. The vote came two days after the city upgraded its heat advisory to a full emergency declaration.

The fund is separate from the arena budget approved earlier this month and does not affect financing for that project, city officials said.
